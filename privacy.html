Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information Layline Advisory Partners (“Layline”, “we”, “us”) collects through laylineadvisory.ai, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers this website and the emails you exchange with us. Work we do for clients is covered by the agreement we sign with each client.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you use the contact form or email us, we receive your name, your email address, your company name if you provide it, and whatever you write in your message. Please leave out passwords, account details and other sensitive information.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, ours records basic technical information when you visit, through our hosting provider. This includes your IP address, browser and device type, the pages requested, and the date and time. We use it to keep the site secure and working.

Cookies and tracking

We do not use advertising cookies or analytics trackers on this site. Our hosting provider may set a strictly necessary cookie to protect the site from automated abuse. Because we do not track visitors across websites, the site behaves the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal.

How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Reply to your enquiry and arrange a conversation
  • Communicate with you about a possible or current engagement
  • Keep the website secure and diagnose problems
  • Meet our legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We will not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

Where the law requires a legal basis for handling personal information, we rely on our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and running our business, on the steps needed to enter into or perform a contract with you, on your consent where we ask for it, and on our legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run this site and our business, and only so they can provide those services:

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and protects it from abuse.
  • Formspree processes contact form submissions and delivers them to our inbox.
  • Zoho provides our email.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on this site. Your browser requests the font files from Google, which receives your IP address when it does.

We may also disclose information to our professional advisers, when the law requires it, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a merger, sale or reorganization of the business.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we are in contact with you, and for a reasonable period afterward for our business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, and we will do so unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Depending on where you live, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California and other US states, you may have further rights. These can include the right to object to or restrict how we use your information, to receive a copy in a portable format, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email jl@laylineadvisory.ai. We may need to confirm your identity first. We will respond within the time the law requires, and we will not treat you differently for making a request.

Where information is processed

We are based in the United States. Your information may be processed in the United States and in other countries where our service providers operate, which may have different data protection laws from the country you live in.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This site is intended for business owners and professionals. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Links to other sites

This site links to other websites, such as LinkedIn. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices or the law change. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to jl@laylineadvisory.ai.
